XY Scope

An oscilloscope-style visualizer for whatever your computer is playing. Everything runs on this page; nothing is uploaded or recorded.

  1. Click Visualize system audio.
  2. In the popup choose the Entire screen tab and click your screen.
  3. Turn on Also share system audio, then click Share.
  4. Click Hide on the “sharing your screen” bar if it’s in the way.

The picture of your screen is thrown away immediately — only the sound is used. Best in Chrome or Edge on Windows.
